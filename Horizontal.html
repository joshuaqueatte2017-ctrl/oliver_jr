Accepted Key Formats

You may provide answers as a continuous sequence such as:

ABCDCDAB

or numbered entries such as:

1 A
2 B
3 C
4 D

Valid answers are A, B, C, and D. Blank values, <, and > are flagged instead of silently being treated as valid answers.
